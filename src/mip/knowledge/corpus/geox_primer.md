# GeoX primer
## Purpose
GeoX is an experiment-oriented approach that compares planned geographic treatment and control conditions.
## Use
Use it when a measurable intervention and defensible geographic design can be defined.
## Limitations
GeoX does not choose treatment markets automatically and does not guarantee lift or feasibility.
## Sources
Derived from MIP GeoX intake and governance contracts. Assignment remains a governed action, not prose guidance.
