# Marketing data requirements
## Purpose
Measurement needs an outcome, dated activity, consistent grain, and enough history and variation to assess readiness.
## Use
Use the requirements conversation to identify fields, controls, geography, and the business question.
## Limitations
Required fields do not guarantee compatibility, fit, causal interpretation, or production readiness.
## Sources
Derived from MIP intake contracts and capability descriptors. Specific missing inputs come from structured workspace truth.
