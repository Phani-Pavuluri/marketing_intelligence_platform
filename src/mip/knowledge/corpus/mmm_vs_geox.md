# MMM versus GeoX
## Purpose
MMM and GeoX answer related but different measurement questions.
## Use
MMM is suited to longitudinal, multi-channel patterns; GeoX is suited to an explicit geographic intervention.
## Limitations
The right method depends on data, intervention, timing, geography, and governance. This guide cannot select markets.
## Sources
Derived from MIP method contracts and capability registry. Readiness and status come from structured truth.
