# Trust, uncertainty, and claims
## Purpose
Trust means matching each claim to its source, provenance, freshness, and verification state.
## Use
Use uncertainty and claim boundaries to distinguish explanation, readiness, evidence, and execution results.
## Limitations
No document can substitute for an artifact, evidence packet, verification result, or release gate.
## Sources
Derived from MIP evidence, verification, and response contracts. Numerical claims require artifact provenance.
