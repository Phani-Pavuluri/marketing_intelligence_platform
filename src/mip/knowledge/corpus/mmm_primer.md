# MMM primer
## Purpose
Marketing mix modeling (MMM) studies relationships between marketing activity, outcomes, and context over time.
## Use
It is useful for broad channel-level planning when history, spend, outcomes, and controls are suitable.
## Limitations
MMM does not automatically prove causality; data quality, variation, confounding, and assumptions matter.
