# Platform Vision

## Purpose

The Marketing Intelligence Platform (MIP) is a **causal marketing intelligence operating system**. It connects experimentation, media mix modeling (MMM), budget planning, and explainable recommendations into a single, inspectable system where statistical engines produce certified outputs and language models orchestrate workflows—not substitute for measurement.

MIP is built for marketing science, finance, and strategy teams who need defensible decisions under uncertainty, not dashboards that summarize last-click metrics or chatbots that invent causal claims.

## What MIP Is

### Causal marketing intelligence operating system

MIP treats marketing measurement as a systems problem: estimands are explicit, engines are versioned, evidence is registered, and recommendations carry contracts (assumptions, diagnostics, uncertainty, unsupported claims). The platform coordinates multiple analytical modalities rather than collapsing them into a single opaque score.

### Experimentation intelligence

Structured ingestion and quality assessment of geo experiments, panel tests, lift studies, and related designs. Experiments serve as **calibration evidence and causal anchors** for models and planning—not as automatic ground truth without compatibility and freshness checks.

### MMM calibration

Reliability-first media mix modeling where **full-panel Δμ (delta-mu)** is the production decision surface. Response curves, channel decomposition, and attribution-style views are diagnostic aids. Experiment evidence feeds calibration loops with explicit compatibility gates.

### Budget planning

Strategic portfolio allocation: how to shift spend across channels, geographies, and time horizons given model surfaces, constraints, and business rules. This is planning and optimization at the marketing mix level—not real-time ad auction bidding or user-level bid shading.

### Explainable recommendations

Recommendations are structured outputs, not prose summaries. Each recommendation exposes evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, diagnostics, confidence tier, and explicit unsupported claims. Users can trace from a suggested action back to engines, inputs, and gates that produced it.

### Conversational orchestration

Natural language is the **control plane** for invoking certified tools, running approved workflows, and explaining results. Orchestration routes to deterministic engines; it does not perform causal estimation, optimization math, or unsupported statistical reasoning in the LLM layer.

## What MIP Is Not

- A generic analytics dashboard or BI replacement
- A chatbot that answers marketing questions from training data
- An autonomous agent that reallocates budget without human gates
- An ad-platform bidding or auction optimization system
- A black-box attribution product

## Long-Term Direction

MIP matures through contracts, reliability harnesses, and phased engine delivery. Conversational interfaces and recommendation engines arrive **after** certified analytical APIs and trust infrastructure exist. Autonomy, where introduced at all, is bounded, gated, and reversible.
